{# The endless field: canvas, detail modal and their script. Included by birds_field.html (whole collection) and by birds.html (the gallery's filtered list), so both share one implementation. Expects: tiles, seq, stride, curate. #} {# ------------------------------------------------------------------ The gallery, unbounded. Same masonry tiles as /birds — natural aspect ratios, no cropping — but laid on a plane that continues in every direction. How it stays true masonry while being infinite: the tile order is a fixed sequence, so the running total of tile heights can be pre-computed once. Column `c` is that same sequence rotated by a stride, so a tile's y is a difference of two prefix sums — O(1), no walking from the origin, and columns pack with no gaps. ------------------------------------------------------------------ #}
