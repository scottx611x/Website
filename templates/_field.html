Same masonry tiles as /birds — natural aspect ratios, no cropping — but laid on a plane that continues in every direction. How it stays true masonry while being infinite: the tile order is a fixed sequence, so the running total of tile heights can be pre-computed once. Column `c` is that same sequence rotated by a stride, so a tile's y is a difference of two prefix sums — O(1), no walking from the origin, and columns pack with no gaps. ------------------------------------------------------------------ #}
